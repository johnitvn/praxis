# Workflow: Research

Systematically investigate a question to produce a well-supported answer.

## Purpose

Research transforms a question into a well-supported answer by gathering, evaluating, and synthesizing evidence. It is the workflow for "I don't know, let me find out."

## When to Use

- You need to answer a question and don't have sufficient knowledge
- You need to understand the state of knowledge on a topic
- You need to evaluate competing claims
- You need to identify what is known and unknown
- The answer requires evidence, not just reasoning

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Question Formulation

**Goal**: Define what you're trying to answer.

1. **State the question precisely**
2. **Decompose into sub-questions** if complex
3. **Identify what kind of answer is needed**: Fact, explanation, evaluation, synthesis
4. **Define scope**: What's in scope, what's out

**Methods**: Problem framing, decomposition

### Phase 2: Scoping

**Goal**: Determine the breadth and depth of research needed.

1. **Assess existing knowledge**: What do you already know?
2. **Identify gaps**: What do you need to find out?
3. **Determine depth**: How thorough does the research need to be?
4. **Set time/effort budget**: How much research is warranted?

**Methods**: Knowledge gap analysis, choose-depth

### Phase 3: Search

**Goal**: Find relevant sources of information.

1. **Identify search strategies**: Where to look
2. **Execute searches**: Multiple queries, multiple sources
3. **Screen results**: Relevance, credibility, recency
4. **Collect promising sources**: Save for evaluation

**Methods**: Information gathering, when-to-search

### Phase 4: Evaluate Sources

**Goal**: Assess the quality and credibility of each source.

1. **Authority**: Who created it? What are their credentials?
2. **Accuracy**: Can the claims be verified?
3. **Objectivity**: Is there bias? What's the agenda?
4. **Currency**: Is it up to date?
5. **Coverage**: How comprehensive is it?

**Methods**: Evidence evaluation, source credibility assessment

### Phase 5: Extract Evidence

**Goal**: Pull relevant information from sources.

1. **Identify key claims and findings**
2. **Note the evidence supporting each claim**
3. **Distinguish facts from interpretations**
4. **Record uncertainties and limitations**

**Methods**: Evidence extraction, argument analysis

### Phase 6: Triangulate

**Goal**: Compare and cross-validate across sources.

1. **Do sources agree?** If not, why not?
2. **Which sources are more credible?**
3. **What patterns emerge across sources?**
4. **What contradictions exist?**

**Methods**: Evidence triangulation, reconciliation

### Phase 7: Synthesize

**Goal**: Combine findings into a coherent answer.

1. **Integrate findings across sources**
2. **Resolve or document contradictions**
3. **Identify the strongest-supported conclusions**
4. **Identify remaining uncertainties**

**Methods**: Evidence synthesis, multi-source synthesis

### Phase 8: Identify Uncertainty

**Goal**: Be honest about what you don't know.

1. **What questions remain unanswered?**
2. **What evidence is weak or missing?**
3. **What assumptions are you making?**
4. **What would change your conclusions?**

**Methods**: Uncertainty quantification, assumption checking

### Phase 9: Report Confidence

**Goal**: Communicate findings with appropriate confidence.

1. **State the answer clearly**
2. **State your confidence level**
3. **Summarize the evidence base**
4. **Note key limitations and uncertainties**
5. **Cite sources**

**Methods**: Confidence calibration, epistemic humility
