# Sources

Comprehensive bibliography for the Praxis reasoning skill.

## Primary Sources by Discipline

### Logic and Formal Reasoning
- Aristotle. (c. 350 BCE). Organon.
- Peirce, C.S. (1931-1958). Collected Papers. Harvard University Press.
- Gentzen, G. (1935). Investigations into logical deduction.
- Tarski, A. (1956). Logic, Semantics, Metamathematics. Oxford University Press.
- Gentner, D. (1983). Structure-mapping: A theoretical framework for analogy. Cognitive Science.
- Allen, J.F. (1983). Maintaining knowledge about temporal intervals. Communications of the ACM.

### Probability and Statistics
- Bayes, T. (1763). An essay towards solving a problem in the doctrine of chances.
- Laplace, P.S. (1814). A Philosophical Essay on Probabilities.
- de Finetti, B. (1937). La prévision: ses lois logiques, ses sources subjectives.
- Jaynes, E.T. (2003). Probability Theory: The Logic of Science. Cambridge University Press.
- Gelman, A. et al. (2013). Bayesian Data Analysis (3rd ed.). CRC Press.
- Fisher, R.A. (1925). Statistical Methods for Research Workers.
- Neyman, J. & Pearson, E. (1933). On the problem of the most efficient tests of statistical hypotheses.

### Causal Reasoning
- Pearl, J. (2000). Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference. Cambridge University Press.
- Pearl, J. & Mackenzie, D. (2018). The Book of Why. Basic Books.
- Spirtes, P., Glymour, C., & Scheines, R. (2000). Causation, Prediction, and Search. MIT Press.
- Rubin, D.B. (1974). Estimating causal effects. Journal of Educational Psychology.
- Morgan, S.L. & Winship, C. (2014). Counterfactuals and Causal Inference. Cambridge.
- Wright, S. (1921). Correlation and causation. Journal of Agricultural Research.

### Decision Theory
- von Neumann, J. & Morgenstern, O. (1944). Theory of Games and Economic Behavior. Princeton.
- Savage, L.J. (1954). The Foundations of Statistics. Wiley.
- Kahneman, D. & Tversky, A. (1979). Prospect theory. Econometrica.
- Keeney, R.L. & Raiffa, H. (1976). Decisions with Multiple Objectives. Wiley.
- Simon, H.A. (1955). A behavioral model of rational choice. QJE.
- Ellsberg, D. (1961). Risk, ambiguity, and the Savage axioms. QJE.

### Systems Thinking
- Meadows, D.H. (2008). Thinking in Systems: A Primer. Chelsea Green.
- Forrester, J.W. (1961). Industrial Dynamics. MIT Press.
- Senge, P.M. (1990). The Fifth Discipline. Doubleday.
- Holland, J.H. (2014). Complexity: A Very Short Introduction. Oxford.
- Barabási, A.L. (2002). Linked: The New Science of Networks. Perseus.
- Sterman, J.D. (2000). Business Dynamics. Irwin/McGraw-Hill.

### Forecasting
- Tetlock, P.E. & Gardner, D. (2015). Superforecasting. Crown.
- Kahneman, D. (2011). Thinking, Fast and Slow. Farrar, Straus and Giroux.

- Silver, N. (2012). The Signal and the Noise. Penguin.
- Mellers, B. et al. (2014). Psychological strategies for winning a geopolitical forecasting tournament. Psych Science.

### Risk Analysis
- Kaplan, S. & Garrick, B.J. (1981). On the quantitative definition of risk. Risk Analysis.
- Taleb, N.N. (2007). The Black Swan. Random House.
- Taleb, N.N. (2012). Antifragile. Random House.

- Shostack, A. (2014). Threat Modeling: Designing for Security. Wiley.
- Vesely, W. et al. (1981). Fault Tree Handbook. U.S. NRC.

### Meta-Cognition
- Flavell, J.H. (1979). Metacognition and cognitive monitoring. American Psychologist.
- Dunlosky, J. & Metcalfe, J. (2009). Metacognition. Sage.
- Kruger, J. & Dunning, D. (1999). Unskilled and unaware of it. JPSP.

- Popper, K. (1959). The Logic of Scientific Discovery. Hutchinson.
- Mayo, D.G. (1996). Error and the Growth of Experimental Knowledge. UChicago Press.

### Design and Creativity

- Brown, T. (2009). Change by Design. HarperBusiness.

### Argumentation
